User Rights

  • Permissions are given to users to access different menus in the application.
  • Mark the Menu or Sub Menu against the user role to provide user, access to that menu or sub menu.


Screen:




Steps:

  1. Click on the Menu.
  2. Permission screen opens.
  3. Check the required menus against user roles to give that role access to those menus.
  4. Click on button to update the permissions given.
