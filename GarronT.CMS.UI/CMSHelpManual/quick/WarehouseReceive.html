Warehouse Receive

  • This screen will be used by warehouse manager to add inventory into the warehouse




Steps

  1. Select project name from the drop down
  2. Select warehouse name from the drop down
  3. Click on icon to scan the product name/part number/UPC code



Or

  1. Enter the product name/part number/UPC code manually in type to search field


Or

  1. Click on button to add the product in to the master if not available


    • Select the utility type of product
    • Select the product category, make, type and size
    • Enter product name, description, part number and UPC code
    • Select serial number radio button to track the product by its serial number
    • Select consumables radio button to track the product by its quantity
    • Tick on Generate barcode checkbox if the barcode needs to be generated for the product
    • Click on button to complete the process





  1. Enter the quantity of product to be received
  2. Tick Generate Barcode checkbox to generate barcode of the product
  3. If the selected product is mapped to serial number button will be displayed


  1. Click on to add the serial numbers of the product


Following screen will open



  1. Message will displayed if user enter duplicate serial number


  1. Click on icon to scan the serial number or enter it manually



  1. Click on Capture button to open the barcode scanner
  2. Click on button once all the serial numbers are entered or scanned.
  3. Click on to add the scanned serial numbers to the grid below



  1. Click on  icon to edit the scanned serial number
  2. Click on icon to delete the scanned serial number
  3. Click on to view status of inventory
  4. Click on to cancel the process
  5. On click of Save button, following screen will open to display status of each inventory



  1. Click on 'Print Barcode' link to open the barcode PDF
  2. Click on Cancel button to cancel the process
  3. Click on Print button to print the barcode
