Warehouse Transfer

  • This screen is used to transfer inventory stock from one warehouse to another under a specific utility and project.


Screen: Warehouse Transfer Form


Steps

  1. Navigate to the Warehouse Transfer page under the Inventory menu.
  2. Select the utility from the Select Utility drop down.
  3. Select the project from the Select Project drop down.
  4. Select the source warehouse in the From Warehouse drop down.
  5. Select the destination warehouse in the To Warehouse drop down.
  6. Select the product to transfer in the Select Product drop down.
  7. OR: Search for the product using the barcode search input by scanning or entering a Barcode, Part number, UPC Code, PO Number, Pallet No, or Tray ID.
  8. Enter the quantity of items to transfer in the Enter Quantity input box.
  9. Check the Generate Barcode checkbox if you wish to print new barcode labels for the transferred stock.
  10. Click on the Add button to stage the items.
  11. If the selected product tracks serial numbers, a modal dialog will automatically pop up prompting you to enter or scan the serial numbers for the quantity entered. Enter the numbers and click Add.
  12. Staged items will appear in the transfer grid with their descriptions, serial numbers, quantities, and status.
  13. You can use the Action column icons to edit quantities, modify serial numbers, or delete items from the staged table.
  14. Once all items are added, click on the Save button at the bottom of the grid to execute the warehouse transfer.
  15. Click on the Cancel button to clear the staged table and reset the transfer form.
