Picture Master

  • This master is used to add picture names.
  • These picture names are displayed in service master in "Picture for the service" dropdown.
  • Picture Names can be edited and deleted through this master.
  • Admin has access to this master.


Screen: Landing Page


Steps

  1. Click on the Menu to open Pictures Landing Page.
  2. It displays list of previously added Picture Names.
  3. Click on button to add new Picture Name.
    • Following screen will open



    • Enter the Picture Name.
    • Click on button to save the picture name.
    • After saving, picture name will be added to the grid on landing page.
    • Click on button to close the form.
    • Click on button to reset all fields.


  1. Click on button to edit corresponding picture detail.
    • Following Screen will open



    • Edit the picture name.
    • Click on button to save the Changes.
    • Click on button to close the form.
