User Receive

  • This screen will be used by installer and manager to receive inventory into their account


Receive by Serial Number




Steps

  1. Select project name from the drop down
  2. By default 'Serial Number' radio button will be checked to receive the inventory by serial number
  3. Enter the serial number manually in the input field and click on to add it into the grid
  4. Click on icon to scan the serial number of product. Following screen will open.



  1. Click on Capture button to open the barcode scanner



  1. Scan multiple serial number one by one
  2. Below screen will display the success and duplicate count
  3. Click on button once all serial numbers are scanned



7. Scanned Serial number will be displayed in below grid



8. Click on  icon to edit the serial number


9. Click on icon to delete the serial number


10. Click on to view status of scanned serial number

11. Click on to cancel the process



Receive by Product Name



Steps

  1. Click on Product Name radio button to receive the inventory by its product name/ part number/ UPC code/ Barcode
  2. Select the receive from i.e warehouse/manager/installer
  3. Select the name of warehouse/manager/installer
  4. Select the utility type from the drop down
  5. Click on  to scan the product name/part number/ UPC code

Or

  1. Enter the product name/part number/ UPC code manually
  2. Enter the quantity to be received in input field
  3. Click on button to add the product into the grid
  4. Click on  icon to edit the product quantity


  1. Click on icon to delete the product


  1. Click on to view status of inventory
  2. Click on to cancel the process
